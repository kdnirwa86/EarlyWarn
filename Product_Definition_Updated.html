2. The 9 Data Sources (Explained Simply)

LAYER 1: OPERATIONAL (What's happening right now?)

Real-time sales, behavior, perception data:

1. GDD Nielsen POS 281 rows

Weekly point-of-sale transactions across retailers. Shows: revenue, volume, market share, distribution by channel.

Answers: "What happened to sales this week? Which channels won/lost?"

2. Circana 1000 (Category Deep-Dive) 1,000 rows

Deodorant-specific weekly data. Shows: subcategory shifts (Aerosol vs Stick), brand competition, format trends.

Answers: "Which format is winning? Are competitors launching new SKUs?"

3. Penetration Panel 2,129 rows

Household behavior monthly. Shows: who's buying, how loyal they are, repeat purchase rates.

Answers: "Are we losing customers? Is loyalty declining?"

4. BGS Brand Health 1,074 rows

Brand perception tracking. Shows: awareness, preference, consideration, emotional connection.

Answers: "Why are customers leaving? Is brand perception falling?"

5. Category Trended (10-Year History) 1,277 rows

Historical baseline. Shows: market growth patterns, price trends, competitive shifts over decade.

Answers: "Is this normal variance or a structural threat?"

LAYER 2: E-COMMERCE (How does online differ?)

6. E-Commerce Data 505 rows

Marketplace prices, availability, reviews. Tracks Amazon, Walmart, Target, Walgreens.

Answers: "Is our online pricing competitive? What do reviews say?"

LAYER 3: COMPETITIVE (What are competitors doing?)

7. Competitive Intelligence 110 rows

Real-time competitor actions: pricing changes, promotions, distribution, new SKU launches.

Answers: "Who's attacking us? What's their strategy?"

LAYER 4: SOCIAL & VOICE (What are consumers saying?)

8. Social Media Sentiment 300 rows

Sentiment tracking across Twitter, Instagram, Reddit, TikTok. Shows trends, complaints, influencer activity.

Answers: "Is brand perception declining online? What's trending?"

LAYER 5: MACRO CONTEXT (What's changing in the world?)

9. Macro Context 100 rows

Economic indicators, regulations, category trends. Shows: inflation, consumer confidence, eco-regulations.

Answers: "Is this a brand threat or a macro economic shift?"

Total Data Available: 7,776 rows across 9 sources
Time Coverage: Weekly (recent) + Monthly (behavior) + Annual (history) + 10-year baseline
Completeness: 100% - all data is validated and ready to use

3. How We Use the 9 Sources

The Signal Generation Process

Each signal is built from MULTIPLE data sources, not one:

Signal Type Primary Source Validation Sources Context
Competitive Price War
Threat: Competitor pricing attack		 Circana (POS prices) E-Commerce, Competitive Intel, Social (sentiment), Macro (inflation) Confirms if real threat or macro-driven
Format Crisis
Threat: Stick losing to Aerosol		 Circana (subcategory trends) E-Commerce (online preferences), Competitive (competitor launches), Social (#aerosolfree trending), Macro (eco-regs) Distinguishes convenience trend vs eco-shift
Penetration Loss
Threat: Fewer households buying		 Penetration Panel (HH %) GDD/Circana (volume down), BGS (awareness?), Social (sentiment), Macro (confidence ↓) Confirms if acquisition or macro issue
Gel Opportunity
Growth: Emerging format		 Circana (format growth) E-Commerce (conversion rate), Social (trending), Panel (segment growth), Competitive (competitor action) Validates market timing + competitive gap
Key Point: We never rely on ONE data source. Every signal gets validated across multiple sources. If they align (94%+ confidence), we surface it. If they conflict, we flag it as uncertain.
